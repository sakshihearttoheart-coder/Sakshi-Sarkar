Meet Your Counselor

Hi, I’m Sakshi — and I believe healing begins when you finally feel safe enough to be yourself.

Through Inner Shift, I hold gentle one-to-one spaces for women who have been carrying too much for too long. Who feel emotionally overwhelmed, stretched too thin, or quietly disconnected from themselves.

Many of the women I work with are strong on the outside — responsible, caring, and constantly giving — yet inside, they feel tired, confused, or unsure of their own needs.

My work is rooted in emotional awareness, self-worth, and learning how to create healthy inner and outer boundaries — not through force or fixing, but through understanding and compassion. I believe that healing doesn’t begin by changing who you are, but by meeting yourself honestly and gently, exactly where you are right now.

In our sessions, you don’t have to perform strength, explain your pain, or rush your healing. This is a space where your emotions are listened to with care, your experiences are respected, and your pace is always honored. I don’t see you as something to be “fixed” — I see you as someone who deserves safety, clarity, and self-trust.

My role is not to tell you who you should be, but to walk beside you as you reconnect with yourself, understand your emotional patterns, and begin choosing from a place of awareness rather than obligation or guilt.

This work is slow, intentional, and deeply human. It’s about learning to feel again without fear, to choose yourself without guilt, and to build a relationship with yourself that feels safe and steady.
