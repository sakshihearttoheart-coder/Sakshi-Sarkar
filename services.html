Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are online counseling sessions as effective as in-person ones?

Yes, absolutely! Online sessions allow you to connect from the comfort and privacy of your own safe space, saving travel time while maintaining full confidentiality and impact.

How do I know how many sessions I will need?

Everyone’s journey is different. While some find immediate clarity in 1–2 single sessions, lasting emotional habits generally develop over a 4-to-8 week period.

Is everything we discuss confidential?

100% yes. Privacy and trust are the absolute foundation of my practice. All communications, notes, and session details remain strictly confidential.
